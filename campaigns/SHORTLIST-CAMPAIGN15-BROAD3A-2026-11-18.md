# Goldmine Hunter — Campaign 15 Shortlist (Broad Wave 3A, 2026-11-18)

*Nine unfired broad-registry 7/10 niches, ranks 28–36 in
registry order, GENERATED from `niches.json` (Atharva's
standing rule: campaigns are generated, not hand-picked).
Method identical to Campaigns 6–10: `campaign15_broad3a.py`
live-crawled 33 surfaces and took Wayback inventories
filtered to statuscode:200 **before** counting;
`verify15.py` / `verify15b.py` then downloaded samples of
every concrete pile (size + md5 + magic in
`outputs/verify15-*.json` / `verify15b-*.json`, files in
`gold/campaign15-verify/`, 3.9 MB total). Grades per
`GRADES.md`; custody per Custody Mapper classes, assigned
post-probe from probe evidence. README and registries
untouched.*

## Headline

**NO GOLD. No silver either: 6 INVENTORY, 3 KILL from nine
7s.** The broad 7-band behaves exactly as the mammal 7-band
did in Campaign 12 — every niche is scientifically real and
every one has already acquired its infrastructure (a formal
platform, a formal archive, a consolidated collection) or
never left paper/plaster. Two niches carry Campaign 12's
*already-run comparison* shape in pure form: the lunar
occultation archive (VI/132C) has itself already re-reduced
1623–present timings against DE440 + Gaia + LOLA — the
registry card's headline — and the orthodontic growth
material was consolidated by AAOF/CGCS, the very growth
centres the niche card excluded. The only loose-file
surfaces in the whole wave are small and deliberate: a
vendor's bat lure-call library (~90 files) and a phage
textbook's host-range tables on a 2004-era society site.

## 1. bat-echolocation-libraries — INVENTORY

The one genuinely loose surface: Avisoft's European bat
lure-call library. The live avisoft.com page 403s this
client and batcalls.com is unreachable from it, but the
Wayback CDX under `avisoft.com/batcalls/*` holds **91
status-200 wav/zip captures (90 wavs)**, and the expanded
mirror at animalsoundlabs.pl serves **53 live audio refs /
63 live data files**. **Sample-verified REAL:** ASL's own
`bb1_x3.wav` live (RIFF/WAVE, 615,066 B, md5 `0367e05d…`)
and a Wayback Avisoft playback wav (RIFF/WAVE, 30,144 B,
md5 `2304af56…`); the `batcalls.zip` pile head carries PK
magic at the 6 MB cap (md5-head `c2ecaceb…`). But the
ChiroVox census prices this pile at **62 records / 26
species** — a vendor's deliberate lure library, published
by design (the ExtractCompare precedent: showcase intent =
INVENTORY, not gold). The field's actual reference
function sits in formal infrastructure: ChiroVox (3,902
recordings, ~200 species, interface-only — 0 direct audio
refs on its home page, restricted calls permission-gated
and documented, never circumvented), Morcegoteca (27
recordings per the same census; its one download-ish link
sampled as a PHANTOM tools page), and TERN's formal portal
(100,000+ Australian calls behind a dashboard). The card's
second ruler (DNA/roost ID) has nothing stranded to bite
on. *Custody: the surface is LOOSE_DIGITAL, the niche is
FORMAL_OR_PLATFORM.*

## 2. butterfly-wing-drawers — INVENTORY

The aggregation the card hoped to find undone has been
done. BioCosmos/LepiVerse lists **619,739 images of
515,141 specimens, 8,401 species, 65 institutions**,
harvested from GBIF, SCAN and Ecdysis; its Zenodo record
was API/crawl-verified (occurrence parquet 40.2 MB +
csv.gz 51.9 MB) and **sample-verified REAL** (manifest
JSON, 2,320 B, md5 `97fa444f…`; columns CSV). AfricaMuseum's
240,000-butterfly collection is digitised through its own
DaRWIN/Virtual Collection + DoeDat transcription project —
formal. BAMONA is a field-photo platform, not specimen
drawers (gallery fetched 200, 102 image refs; its sampled
image was site furniture, recorded as such). No regional
museum or society drawer pile surfaced loose anywhere.
*Custody: FORMAL_OR_PLATFORM → CAUTION; the silver ceiling
was not reached because the occurrence tables complete no
vintage stack — they are the current platform's own
export.*

## 3. snake-venom-spectra — INVENTORY

The card's premise — "raw runs rarely leave the lab site"
— is dated. They leave into formal deposits, at scale:
**API-verified** Zenodo *Vipera berus* (4 files,
3,185,984,125 B), *V. monticola* 3 subspecies (4 files,
8,992,758,904 B) and *V. ammodytes* 7 island/mainland pools
(8 files, 20,580,019,645 B), plus PRIDE PXD016503 (live
200) and the MassIVE Türkiye seven-taxon set. **Sample-
verified REAL:** both deposits' UniProt reference fastas
(726,279 B, md5 `0978f197…`; 734,737 B, md5 `ee636ac5…` —
both md5s match the values Zenodo itself publishes), and
PK magic on the berus RAW zip head. Everything is
published by design, and no deposit carries the card's
second ruler (antivenom neutralisation), so the headline
comparison cannot run on these sets alone. *Custody:
FORMAL_OR_PLATFORM → CAUTION.* **Pair Hunter flag (the
wave's best):** the deposits share species / population /
site keys across the *Vipera* complex and reuse identical
reference fastas — a cross-deposit population re-analysis
is joinable today on published data, and an antivenom-
efficacy table surface, if one is ever found loose, pairs
against all of them at once.

## 4. heritage-tree-ring-cores — KILL

No ring-width series file exists at any host probed. The
ADS VAG Dendrochronology Database (DOI 10.5284/1132568,
4,000+ building records) holds **dates and descriptions
only** — its record pages carry zero data-file refs and
its Wayback data-filter CDX returns **0**. Historic
England's tree-ring output is a formal report-PDF series
(the series are printed inside reports, never served as
`.rwl`/`.txt`); the labs' own series stay with their
client reports. *Stated plainly:* both HE and ADS pages
were unreachable from this client on every attempt
(connection broken / invalid chunk length, three tries
across two scripts) — the verdict rests on the CDX zero,
the search-indexed record/report text, and the absence of
any series file at any reachable surface, and a browser-
equipped pass could re-check the two pages; it could not
create a series pile that the CDX says was never there.
*Custody: PAPER_ONLY / lab-held → KILL. The koala class:
a perfect fidelity card whose population never became
files.*

## 7. lunar-occultation-timings — INVENTORY

The archive exists, is formal, and has already done the
headline work. VizieR **VI/132C** (Herald & Gault) brings
the 1623–present timings together "in a consistent
format" with reductions based on **JPL DE440, LOLA limb
corrections and Gaia EDR3 star positions** — i.e. the
card's "re-reduce the 1970s timings against modern
satellite positions" comparison was run by the archive
itself in its 2022 edition (the Campaign 12 already-run
shape). The section site's loose remainder is software
distribution, published by design: lunar-occultations.com
holds **49–53 status-200 data files** (Occult4 observation
zips, site lists), **sample-verified REAL** (`eurosit.dat`,
257 B, md5 `f3578d3c…`), and IOTA's current RASC lunar PDF
is predictions, not timings (REAL, 768,476 B, md5
`3025b344…`). CDS itself was unreachable from this client
on every attempt (connection aborted, http and https) —
the VI/132C facts above are from the GAVO registry record
and the archive's own description, stated as such.
*Custody: FORMAL_OR_PLATFORM → CAUTION.*

## 8. observatory-plate-logbooks — INVENTORY (documented surface)

The flagship *and* the long tail were formalised together.
APPLAUSE DR3 (plate-archive.org documentation fetched
live, 200) holds **101,138 scans of 70,276 plates, 96,852
logbook-page/plate-cover images and 215 logbooks** across
24 archives — Hamburg, Bamberg, Potsdam, Tautenburg,
Tartu, Vatican — with plate-solving (PyPlate/Gaia) already
applied, which is also the card's headline comparison,
run. WFPDB (VizieR VI/90) holds the small-observatory
metadata layer: 323,635 observations from 57 catalogues.
skyarchive.org (WFPDB home) is live (200). No small
observatory serves a loose logbook-PDF pile of its own at
any host probed. Under the standing evidence rule the
grade is INVENTORY claimed only as a documented surface
(the Campaign 10 fire-insurance treatment): APPLAUSE is a
TAP/database interface, so no plate or logbook data file
could be sampled from this client, and CDS was
unreachable throughout. *Custody: FORMAL_OR_PLATFORM →
CAUTION.*

## 9. early-radio-astronomy-charts — KILL

The charts were never scanned, anywhere probed. What
exists on the web is (a) formal digitisation of a
*different* record — Manchester Library's Jodrell Bank
**logbooks** project (instrument-configuration books,
1968–, page fetched 200 with 11 collection links), the
observatory's archive being John Rylands Library custody;
and (b) published narrative about the charts, **sample-
verified REAL**: Bell Burnell's Cosmic Search account of
analyzing 96 feet of pen-chart paper a day by hand (PDF,
219,993 B, md5 `ae235ef2…`) and the Cosmic Search Reber
history (PDF, 605,705 B, md5 `14e1ee2b…`). The Cambridge
IPS charts, the Jodrell drift records and their peers sit
in observatory archives as paper. *Custody: PAPER_ONLY →
KILL. A niche whose web presence is writing about the
objects — the photo-finish shape.*

**Pattern update, fifth broad wave:** at score 7 the
failure is no longer only infrastructure or custody — it
is *completion*. Twice in nine niches (lunar, plates) the
formal system had not merely ingested the data but
already run the registry card's headline comparison
against the modern anchor (Gaia/LOLA; PyPlate/Gaia). A
pre-flight "has the comparison been run?" literature check
— the Campaign 11 teeth lesson — would have cut both
cards on paper. The wave's one transferable asset is a
Pair Hunter lead, not a pile: the *Vipera* venom deposits
share population keys and reference fastas across four
formal archives, joinable today, missing only their
antivenom ruler.

## What we did NOT do

- No bulk pulls: total new disk **3.9 MB** of samples. The
  venom deposits (32.8 GB combined) were inventoried via
  the Zenodo API and sampled at their smallest files only;
  the batcalls.zip pile was head-sampled at a 6 MB cap,
  never harvested.
- Permission systems (ChiroVox restricted-call requests,
  AIATSIS-style community gating analogues, AAOF's
  interface) were documented as custody, never scraped or
  circumvented.
- Client-blocked surfaces (avisoft.com 403, batcalls.com
  unreachable, CDS aborted, HE/ADS chunk errors, AAOF 403)
  are reported as this client's results, not as absences —
  every KILL above rests on at least one reachable
  positive surface (CDX inventories, registry records, or
  sampled narrative/catalogue files), named in its section.
- README and registries not edited; no git commit — the
  parent handles those.
