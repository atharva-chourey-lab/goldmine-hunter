## 1. Morpho-CT / Rhino-5 — LANDED PATTERN (closest to lion, 7.5–8)
Report: `fidelity-morpho-report.md` · Bundle: `gold/morpho-ct/`
Mallet 2019 Supp. 9 (Europe PMC, barrier-free, MD5 verified) holds all
5 living rhinos, same protocol, same 6 long bones. The centroid-size
profile (within-specimen cross-bone proportions — never published by
Mallet) separates all 10 pairs (p ≤ 0.04); largest Diceros bicornis vs
R. unicornis 0.0566, p=0.0014; same-genus R. sondaicus vs R. unicornis
0.0458, p=0.0011. Forearm (radius+ulna) carries the signal.
Limits: 5-way extension was post-hoc, not separately pre-registered —
register before any publication claim. Expansion to other taxa PARKED
at Atharva's direction until the brain trio (dolphin / brain-rhino /
ferret) lands and he sees the whole board.

## 2. Gaia chemistry — LANDED CONTRAST, crowded field
Report: `fidelity-gaia-report.md` · Bundle: `gold/gaia-chemistry/`
Gaia TAP barrier-free: GSP-Phot [M/H] for 470.8M sources, GSP-Spec for
5.55M. GALAH DR3 mirror found and parsed (16,120 rows; 7,698 quality
stars, all joined to Gaia). First contrast (Gaia − GALAH [Fe/H]):
overall −0.062 dex hides a slope — +1.85 dex at [Fe/H] −3..−2, +1.45
at −2..−1.5, +0.93 at −1.5..−1, −0.18 at solar; giants +0.17 vs dwarfs
−0.15. APOGEE blocked today on all routes (SDSS hosts) — smallest
unblock is one live-browser fetch. Novelty is narrow: global biases
are published; unclaimed is the joint Teff × logg × extinction ×
[Fe/H] map on the independent GALAH anchor + the very-metal-poor tail.

## 3. Cryo-EM (EMDB × PDB × AlphaFold) — SET COMPLETE, pattern not yet run
Report: `fidelity-cryoem-report.md` · Bundle: `gold/cryoem/`
All access barrier-free, 3 maps downloaded byte-exact and parsed.
Measured set: 37,885 map+model entries; ~10–15k maps with no model;
12/12 sampled with-model entries also have an AlphaFold prediction.
First check (one entry): map vs deposited model agree ~1 Å/axis;
AlphaFold monomer vs the tetramer in the map is the gap in numbers.
Unclaimed: population census including the no-model maps (existing
audits filter them out) + joint local-resolution × pLDDT disagreement
geometry. No population pattern computed yet — potential, not a find.

Gaia second: a completed contrast, dramatic at the metal-poor end,
but the field has already published the global version.
Cryo-EM third: the largest complete set, but only a set — the
pattern analysis is the next build, on Atharva's call.
