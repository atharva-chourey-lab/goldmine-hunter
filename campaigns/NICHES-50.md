# The 50 — niche-keyword registry (2026-11-18)

Atharva's commission: ~50 niche domain keywords, not touched as much but
with gold potential — headline-shaped like mammals (intuitive to anyone,
hunter-level scope underneath) — targeted by Goldmine Hunter and its
tooling. Campaigns are now GENERATED from the top of this registry
(see README, 'Design doctrine — niche keywords').

**Selection tests (every entry passed all three on paper):**
(a) *Fidelity* — raw capture kept; one protocol over dozens+ comparable
units; labels gapped, stranded or on a second ruler; a local anchor.
(b) *Headline* — a non-specialist gets the comparison in one sentence.
(c) *Fragmentation* — NOT a mature field whose central product is already
reconciliation (those publish by design: CRU, Reptile, ATNF — Campaign 5).

Excluded: everything already hunted, landed or killed (Brain Catalogue /
mammal brains, rhino forearm, Maddison, CRU, Gaia/GALAH, cryo-EM, cosmology
sims, HCA, WorldClim, SILSO, SDSS, and all Campaign 2–5 hosts). Adjacent-
but-distinct entries are marked as such in their why-niche line.

Machine-readable twin: `niches.json`. In the hunter: `python3
goldmine_hunter.py niches [--top N | --niche <slug>]`; per-niche gold
words + signature extensions feed `niche_score()`.

## Full cards

### A. Animal bodies & senses

#### Fish otolith (ear-stone) image & chemistry archives — `fish-otoliths` — score 9/10 (rank 1)
- **Score justification:** Raw images + chemistry, dozens of lab collections, reader-vs-reader age gap, known-age anchors exist.
- **Keywords:** `otolith image archive jpg`; `sagitta otolith database download`; `otolith age reading csv xls`; `otolith microchemistry transect data`
- **Signature files:** .jpg, .tif, .csv, .xls · **Gold words:** otolith, sagitta, annuli, microchem
- **RAW:** High-resolution otolith photographs and LA-ICP-MS / isotope transect files on lab and institute sites
- **UNIT:** One fish specimen — hundreds per collection, dozens of species, same imaging protocol
- **LABEL GAP / SECOND RULER:** Age read by reader A vs reader B vs chemistry; usually only published means survive
- **ANCHOR:** Known-age hatchery fish and bomb-radiocarbon validated ages
- **HEADLINE SHAPE:** A stone inside a fish's head has recorded every winter of its life — and two experts reading it can disagree by years.
- **Why niche/untouched:** Age labs keep their own image piles; there is no central reconciliation product, only per-lab atlases (AFORO, national collections) that never joined each other's readings.

#### Weakly electric fish EOD waveform recordings — `electric-fish-eod` — score 9/10 (rank 2)
- **Score justification:** Raw .wav is the specimen; species-specific signal; taxonomy rewritten by DNA after recordings were filed by ear.
- **Keywords:** `electric organ discharge wav download`; `mormyrid EOD recordings archive`; `gymnotiform EOD waveform data`; `electric fish finder mp3 recordings`
- **Signature files:** .wav, .mp3, .csv · **Gold words:** eod, discharge, waveform, mormyrid, gymnotiform
- **RAW:** Uncompressed .wav/.mp3 electric-organ-discharge recordings with oscillogram exports, on lab and personal research sites
- **UNIT:** One recorded fish — hundreds of individuals, dozens of species per expedition, one electrode protocol
- **LABEL GAP / SECOND RULER:** Species named by waveform at capture vs later DNA-barcode revisions of the same vouchers
- **ANCHOR:** Museum voucher specimens tied to recording numbers
- **HEADLINE SHAPE:** A fish that 'sees' with electricity broadcasts its own name in a waveform — and the recordings were filed under names DNA later changed.
- **Why niche/untouched:** A handful of labs (Cornell mormyrids, Crampton's gymnotiforms) hold expedition piles on their own sites; no cross-lab EOD library exists.

#### Cephalopod beak collections from predator stomachs — `cephalopod-beaks` — score 8/10 (rank 9)

- **Keywords:** `cephalopod beak measurements csv`; `squid beak identification guide pdf`; `octopus beak morphometrics data`; `beak collection stomach contents database`
- **Signature files:** .csv, .xls, .jpg · **Gold words:** beak, rostral, cephalopod, morphometr
- **RAW:** Beak photographs and rostral-length measurement tables from whale, seal and albatross stomach-content studies
- **UNIT:** One beak — thousands per predator-sampling programme, dozens of prey species, one measuring key
- **LABEL GAP / SECOND RULER:** Prey species ID from beak keys vs DNA metabarcoding of the same stomachs (second ruler)
- **ANCHOR:** Whole-specimen beak-to-body regressions from museum material
- **HEADLINE SHAPE:** The only part of a giant squid that survives a whale's stomach is its beak — and the beak remembers how big the squid was.
- **Why niche/untouched:** Stomach-content programmes publish summary diet percentages; the beak-by-beak measurement piles stay on lab sites and in thesis appendices.

#### Bivalve shell growth-ring (sclerochronology) image archives — `bivalve-shell-rings` — score 8/10 (rank 10)
- **Score justification:** Cross-section images are raw chronologies; each lab reads its own shells; isotope profiles are an independent ruler.
- **Keywords:** `sclerochronology shell image archive`; `clam shell growth increments data`; `bivalve cross section photograph collection`; `shell ring chronology csv`
- **Signature files:** .jpg, .tif, .csv · **Gold words:** sclerochron, increment, shell, chronology

- **UNIT:** One shell — hundreds per site chronology, same sectioning and imaging protocol
- **LABEL GAP / SECOND RULER:** Ring-count ages vs oxygen-isotope years measured on the same shell (second ruler)
- **ANCHOR:** Known-collection-date shells and crossdated master chronologies
- **HEADLINE SHAPE:** A clam on the seafloor has been keeping a yearly diary in its shell longer than most countries have existed.
- **Why niche/untouched:** Sclerochronology is a small community of labs with personal chronology pages; the image archives behind the published chronologies were never pooled.
