## Mantidactylus betsileanus — daughter geography (for Stage 1's provisional call)

ASW taxon page for *Mantidactylus betsileanus* (Boulenger, 1882),
read 2026-11-18:
https://amphibiansoftheworld.amnh.org/Amphibia/Anura/Mantellidae/Mantellinae/Mantidactylus/Mantidactylus-betsileanus

- The post-revision (Scherz et al. 2022, Megataxa 7:113–311)
  distribution of *M. betsileanus* s.s. is given as: "Widespread
  over a large area of central to southeastern Madagascar …
  known from the vicinity of Lac Alaotra, Ambatovaky, Ambodisakoa,
  Ambohitantely, **Andasibe**, Anjozorobe, Anosibe An'Ala, Antara,
  Antsirakambiaty forest, Befanjana, Betampona, Fierenana,
  Fivahona, Itremo, Mahasoa, **Mandraka**, Maromizaha,
  Marotandrano-Riamalandy, Namoly, Ranomafana and surrounds,
  Sahambaky Forest (Lakato), Torotorofotsy, Tsaranoro, and
  Tsinjoarivo. Elevation range: 190–1648 m."
- Both localities in the Fonozoo catalogue envelope for this name
  (Andasibe ×4, Mandraka ×3) therefore fall inside the s.s.
  distribution as circumscribed by the splitting revision itself.
- Counterweight on the same page: the ASW comment records that
  Glaw & Vences (2007) noted "similar unnamed species from …
  5) Andasibe" — an unnamed candidate lineage was historically
  noted at one of the two envelope localities (pre-revision note;
  the 2022 revision's own account, ASW pp. 213–217, treats
  Andasibe material under betsileanus s.s.).
- The ASW page for the principal northern daughter,
  *Mantidactylus noralottae*, could not be fetched in this session
  (browser fetch timed out, 2026-11-18; not retried per failure
  protocol), so sympatry of a daughter at Andasibe/Mandraka was
  not independently excluded from ASW in this pass.

## Access note (methods-relevant)

ASW's only public access is per-name web search and per-taxon
pages (structure verified 2026-11-18: taxon pages carry full
synonymy, distribution and geographic occurrence). No API, no
bulk download, no checklist export is offered. Any large-scale
"ASW check" in the literature is therefore necessarily mediated —
here, by the Catalogue of Life (whose amphibian taxonomy derives
from ASW) — and this mediation is declared wherever the
cross-check is reported.
