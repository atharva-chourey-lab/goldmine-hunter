# Spin-off: Fonozoo Re-identification — EXHAUSTED (full results)

Minted from Goldmine Hunter **Campaign 6 / Wave 1** (2026-11-18;
`campaigns/SHORTLIST-CAMPAIGN6-WAVE1-2026-11-18.md`), the
registry-generated campaign whose #1 seed (frog-call archives) produced
the hunter's first registry **GOLD**: 1,013 status-200 audio files
stranded on fonozoo.com, in no formal archive, sample-verified.

The minted comparison has now been **run to exhaustion** and written
up as a manuscript (published separately as the lab's
`fonozoo-reidentification` manuscript repository, CC BY 4.0, not yet
peer-reviewed). Headline: of 629 countable species-named recordings,
**146 (23.2%) no longer carry their filed name** in the GBIF Backbone
Taxonomy (129 genus recombinations, 15 junior synonyms, 2 split
elevations), 6 are split-ambiguous (4 resolved by joining the
institution's own catalogue), and the maintained FNJV control archive
changed at only 1.1% — the dead-name burden is a property of filing
state, not era. Contents: `MANUSCRIPT.md`, `RESULTS-EXHAUSTED.md`
(superseding the earlier `RESULTS.md`), `NOVELTY.md`,
`PREREGISTRATION.md`, full per-recording tables in `data/`, and run
summaries in `results/`. The recovered audio files remain the
Fonoteca Zoológica's (MNCN-CSIC) and are not redistributed.
