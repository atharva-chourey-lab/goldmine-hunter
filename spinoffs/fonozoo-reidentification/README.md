# Spin-off: Fonozoo Re-identification

Minted from Goldmine Hunter **Campaign 6 / Wave 1** (2026-11-18;
`campaigns/SHORTLIST-CAMPAIGN6-WAVE1-2026-11-18.md`), the
registry-generated campaign whose #1 seed (frog-call archives) produced
the hunter's first registry **GOLD**: 1,013 status-200 audio files
stranded on fonozoo.com, in no formal archive, sample-verified. The
spin-off ran the comparison the niche card had pre-named:
re-identifying the accessioned advertisement calls — filed under
recording-era species names — against the current GBIF backbone
taxonomy. Result: of 629 countable species-named recordings, 146
(23.2%) changed name (129 genus moves, e.g. *Hyla*→*Boana*), 6 are
split-ambiguous and were flagged rather than guessed, 469 stand;
pre-registered scorecard 3/6. Contents: `RESULTS.md`, `NOVELTY.md`,
`PREREGISTRATION.md`, `results/summary.json` + `corrections.json`,
and the full per-name tables in `data/` (`classifications.csv`,
`inventory.csv`, `resolutions.csv`).
