# CRU Vintages — novelty scrub (2026-11-18, before any analysis)

Method: `research_novelty_scrub` skill. Claim stated first, split,
each part graded against the closest prior found in the primary
literature and CRU's own record.

## The claim as commissioned (one sentence)

"A systematic version-vs-version comparison of CRUTEM3, CRUTEM4 and
CRUTEM5 — global and hemispheric annual series — measuring how each
successive version revised the land temperature record, has not been
published."

## Split and graded

**A. CRUTEM4 vs CRUTEM3 differences (global/hemispheric means, trends,
spatial patterns) — ALREADY PUBLISHED.**
Closest prior: Jones, P.D. et al. (2012), "Hemispheric and large-scale
land-surface air temperature variations: an extensive revision and an
update to 2010", *JGR Atmospheres* — the CRUTEM4 release paper.
Its §4 is an extensive CRUTEM4-vs-CRUTEM3 comparison: NH warmer by
~0.1 °C since 2001 from added Arctic/Russian stations; NH/SH warming
1.12/0.84 °C over 1901–2010; spatial difference maps; trend-difference
maps. Secondary coverage (Skeptical Science's CRUTEM4 detailed look)
adds that >⅓ of the recent global difference is the *global-averaging
convention change* (CRUTEM3: equal-weighted hemispheres; CRUTEM4:
land-area-weighted hemispheres), not new data.

**B. CRUTEM5 vs CRUTEM4 global-mean change — ALREADY PUBLISHED (in
summary form).**
Closest prior: Osborn, T.J. et al. (2021), "Land surface air
temperature variations across the globe updated to 2019: the CRUTEM5
dataset", *JGR Atmospheres* 126, e2019JD032352 — the CRUTEM5 release
paper. Verdict in its abstract: the global-mean time series is "only
slightly modified compared with previous versions"; warming
1861–1900 → 2015–2019 is 1.6 °C (1.7 °C with the alternative
high-latitude gridding, itself a +~0.1 °C effect). Regional changes
are shown; a year-by-year 5−4 revision series is not tabulated.

**C. HadCRUT5 vs HadCRUT4 global/hemispheric differences — ALREADY
PUBLISHED, and out of scope here.**
Morice, C.P. et al. (2021), the HadCRUT5 release paper, Fig. 1 plots
HadCRUT5-minus-HadCRUT4 global and hemispheric difference series —
but HadCRUT is land+ocean: most of that difference is HadSST4 and
infilling, not CRUTEM. It does not do the CRUTEM-only geometry.

**D. CRU TS minor-release comparisons — PUBLISHED BY THE PRODUCER,
per release.**
CRU TS release notes (e.g. v4.05, v4.06, v4.07, v4.08) ship
release-vs-release comparison plots/PDFs, and Harris, I. et al.
(2020, *Scientific Data* 7:109) documents CRU TS v4 against v3.
Per-release, producer-side, mostly graphical — not an independent
systematic treatment, and CRU TS is a different product (0.5°
multivariate grids from 1901), held out of Phase 1 scope regardless.

**E. The full 3→4→5 lineage as ONE uniform revision-geometry
treatment — NARROWED BUT OPEN.**
What no prior found does: take all three pairwise steps (4−3, 5−4,
5−3) through a single uniform framework — year-by-year revision
series, decade means of revisions, period-trend revisions
(1850–1900 / 1901–1950 / 1951–2000 / 2001–end), endpoint-warming
change per step, and a concentration accounting (which eras hold the
revision mass) — for global + NH + SH, in the Maddison-vintages
sense of a revision *geometry*. Jones et al. (2012) covers step 4−3
in release-paper form; Osborn et al. (2021) summarises step 5−4 in
one sentence and an endpoint number; nobody chains the three steps,
decomposes 5−3 into its two steps, or runs the concentration
accounting. Widened searches ("CRUTEM version differences decade
trends revision analysis systematic") returned only the two release
papers and blog commentary — no third-party systematic treatment.

## Surviving claim (shrunk to the seam)

Not "first comparison of CRUTEM versions" — that is dead, killed by
Jones et al. (2012) and Osborn et al. (2021). The surviving claim is:

**The first (to the scrub's knowledge) uniform revision-geometry
accounting of the full CRUTEM 3→4→5 lineage: all three pairwise
revision series, decade and period-trend revision structure, and
revision-mass concentration, computed identically for global
(harmonised), NH and SH annual land series.**

"No first claim beyond this wording is licensed by this scrub."
